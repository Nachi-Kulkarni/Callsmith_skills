## Real delta formula

```
value_gained − trust_cost − setup_friction − switching_anxiety − maintenance_burden
```

| Tax | Drive toward zero |
|---|---|
| Trust | Schema-gated packs, **no synthesis**, floor rewrites, binary evals |
| Setup | Skill-first install; optional thin CLI |
| Switching | Open packs + agent-written contracts; MIT |
| Maintenance | No scaffold to fight; packs/floors/eval compound |
