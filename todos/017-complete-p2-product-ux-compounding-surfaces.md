# Product UX and compounding agent surfaces

## Problem Statement

The product claims irreversible value through contract history, shared standards, and repeated learning, but generated contracts are ignored and the repository currently demonstrates no closed proof loop.

## Findings

The main skill is repetitive, product doctrine is duplicated, and hooks/plugins/workflows remain conceptual rather than thin adapters over one canonical knowledge core.

## Proposed Solutions

1. Add more standalone surfaces and duplicate instructions: maintenance burden.
2. Keep one canonical skill/reference core, make committed contract/receipt/trace artifacts the workflow, and provide minimal adapter guidance: recommended.

## Recommended Action

Create a five-minute before/after journey, committed contract workflow, validation receipt, concise skill with progressive references, and clear cross-agent installation/CI usage.
