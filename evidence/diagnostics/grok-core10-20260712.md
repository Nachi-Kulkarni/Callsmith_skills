# Grok-4.5 core10 diagnostic

Same briefs. Same model. Same reasoning level. Same budgets. The only planned difference was whether
the coding agent could use Callsmith.

## Result

Across 29 valid paired trials, Callsmith moved complete task success from **10.3% to 100%**: a
**+89.7 percentage-point lift**. The 95% paired-bootstrap interval was +75.9 to +100.0 points.

| Gate | BASE | WITH | Lift |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Complete task success | 10.3% | 100% | +89.7 pp |
| Floors | 62.1% | 100% | +37.9 pp |
| Provider physics | 10.3% | 100% | +89.7 pp |
| Structured contract | 10.3% | 100% | +89.7 pp |
| Reality traps | 48.3% | 100% | +51.7 pp |

Success required all four gates. A good average could not hide a missing safety floor, impossible
audio path, malformed contract, or hard reality veto.

## What changed in the artifacts

### Clinic: plausible prose was not a safe design

BASE wrote a polished clinic plan, but the machine receipt still contained an invalid retention
value, a handoff below the required medical floor, an unknown TTS provider, and the wrong contract
shape. It failed all four gates.

WITH rewrote the policy fields into accepted values, used known provider packs, and bound the choices
into a validated receipt. It passed all four gates.

### Exotel: naming the bridge was not enough

BASE recognized Exotel and a custom FastAPI bridge, yet its final artifacts did not satisfy the
custom-transform physics or structured-contract checks.

WITH made bridge ownership and transforms explicit in the design and kept the answers and contract
consistent. It passed every gate in the valid Exotel pairs.

### WhatsApp: voice does not automatically mean PSTN

BASE produced a plausible asynchronous triage design, but its answers and contract disagreed about
the surface, handoff, and provider reality.

WITH classified the surface as WhatsApp voice notes, removed telephony, disabled full-duplex
barge-in, and recorded the medical retention and escalation decisions in the receipt.

## The result that did not work

One scheduled arm—trial 2, `exotel-custom-transform-trap`, WITH—ended normally but left
`voice.answers.json` untouched. The validity gate caught it. That makes the complete run invalid:
29/30 pairs were valid, `run_valid` is false, and `publishable` is false.

This failure will not be retried away. A replacement chosen after seeing the failure would bias the
result. The next publication candidate must be a fresh, complete, predeclared run.

## Where Callsmith did not add lift

BASE already passed in three valid pairs: `clinic-implement-golden` trial 1, `bank-kyc` trial 2, and
`whatsapp-not-pstn` trial 3. Those no-lift results remain part of the estimate.

Callsmith also added a small median actor-time cost: 58.1 seconds for WITH versus 54.0 seconds for
BASE. WITH was slower in 13 valid pairs and faster in 16. No token or monetary comparison is claimed
because the required provider usage receipts were not present.

## Status

This is strong single-family diagnostic evidence. It is not Callsmith's release claim. Publication
still requires a complete valid core10 run, sanitized checksummed receipts, and replication under a
second eligible model family with identical frozen controls.
